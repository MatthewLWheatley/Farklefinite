Explain How to win/lose


Reach the goal score before you run out of lives.

Each turn starts with rolling all 6 dice. After each roll, you must:
Select dice that score points

Choose to either:
Set Aside: Lock in those dice, roll the remaining dice, and try to score more
Bank: Take your current score and end your turn (costs 1 life unless you got Hot Dice)

If you roll and nothing scores, you Farkle you lose everything you built up that turn AND lose a life.

Scoring (the only part that matters):

Straight 1-6 = 5000
Straight 1-5 or 2-6 = 2500
Three matching pairs = 1500
Three of a kind: 1s give 1000, 
everything else is pip × 100 (so three 4s = 400)
Four/five/six of a kind multiply by 2 for each extra die
Single 1s = 100 each
Single 5s = 50 each

Start with 5 lives
Bank = lose a life 
Farkle = lose running score AND a life
Hot dice = if you set aside or bank all 6 dice scoring, you bank for free and roll all 6 again

On Round end you will gain money equal to lives 
+ set a side score divided by goal score (capped at 5)
